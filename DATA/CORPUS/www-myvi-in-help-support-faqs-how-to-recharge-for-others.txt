Recharge &amp; Balance
How to Recharge for Others?
From the
Vi App
homepage click on "Recharge for Others"
Enter the Vi prepaid number and select a suitable pack
Proceed to pay and complete your transaction
The selected pack will be activated for the Vi number
Do not have the Vi App? Worry not, you can quickly recharge via the Vi website without logging in.
Click on "
Mobile Recharge
" from the prepaid menu
Enter the prepaid number and amount
Or browse through and select a suitable pack
Click on "buy pack" and complete your payment
The selected recharge will be activated shortly
