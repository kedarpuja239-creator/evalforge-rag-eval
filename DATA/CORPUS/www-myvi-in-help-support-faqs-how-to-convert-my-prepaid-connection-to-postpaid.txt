New to Vi
I want to convert my Prepaid to Postpaid connection
While upgrading to Vi postpaid, all Vi prepaid users can carry their prepaid balance to postpaid.
Follow these steps to convert your Prepaid SIM to Postpaid online
Go to the
Vi App
/website, and click on "Upgrade to Postpaid"
Select a suitable
postpaid plan
and click on proceed
Fill in your contact details and address for
free SIM home delivery
Select a
fancy number
of your choice or continue with the existing number
Get OTP to complete your order!
Call on 59059 to activate your
new SIM
Alternatively, you can also visit your nearest Vi store to switch from a Prepaid to a Postpaid connection.
Please carry your
valid Identity Proof and Address Proof
with you. To find your nearest Vi store,
Click here
.
