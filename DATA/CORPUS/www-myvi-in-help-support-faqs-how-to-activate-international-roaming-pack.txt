I want to activate International roaming pack
You can activate your international roaming pack via the Vi App or website.
1) Go to the
International Roaming page
and enter your Vi number
2) Select your visiting country/countries and the travel duration
3) Choose from customized travel packs based on your inputs and click on "buy now"
4) Complete your transaction to activate International roaming services for your number
