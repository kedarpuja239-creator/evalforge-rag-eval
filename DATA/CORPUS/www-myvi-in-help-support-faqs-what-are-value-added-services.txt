<b>What are Value Added Services?</b>
Apart from telco benefits like unlimited calls and data, Vi offers several additional value-added services for your non-stop entertainment! Download the Vi
App
and conveniently activate or deactivate value-added services like:
Vi Movies & TV
: Access 400+ live TV channels, movies, and leading OTT platforms like Voot Select, Yupp TV, and more through the Vi App
Callertunes
: Entertain callers while they wait for you to answer the phone. Select from a song or movie dialogue in a language of your choice
Name tunes
: Search and set ringtones with your name in it to amuse callers
Profile tunes: Keep callers informed of your current status when you are occupied and cannot answer the call. Choose from different profile tunes and set the duration of the tune
Star Talk Pack: Live chat with your favorite celebrities, or listen in to conversations
Contest Pack: Answer questions and win exciting prizes
Sports Pack: Stay up to date with your favorite sports or connect with the sports celebrities
Conference Call: Add multiple people to a phone call and talk with up to 5 people simultaneously
Live Astrology: Connect to an AstroBuddy and get answers to all your queries
Missed Call Information Service (MCI): SMS alerts for calls missed while your phone was switched off
Voice Mail Service
: Divert calls to your voice mailbox when your phone is switched off and get SMS alerts when someone leaves a voice message
