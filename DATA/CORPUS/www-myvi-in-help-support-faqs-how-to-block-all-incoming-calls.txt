<b>How To Block All Incoming Calls?</b>
You can filter out unwanted calls and get rid of irritating callers. Here is how to block incoming calls:
Use the USSD code *717#
Or go to your phone settings and navigate to the Do Not Disturb section, click on “Allow calls from”, and select the option “No One”. This will prevent any incoming calls to your
mobile number
.
