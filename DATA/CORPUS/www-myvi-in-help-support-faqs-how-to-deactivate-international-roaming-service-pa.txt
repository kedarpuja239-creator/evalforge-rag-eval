Roaming
I want to deactivate international roaming service/packs
1) To deactivate your international roaming service/pack, please download the
Vi App
and deactivate the same by going to Main Menu >> Active packs and services >> Packs/Services.
2) To deacivate your international roaming service, you need to send a message CAN IR to 199 toll free.
