How to activate/deactivate value added services (VAS)?
Vi offers additional value-added services such as callertunes, name tunes or profile tunes, contest packs, etc. To activate/deactivate
value-added services
(VAS):
Download the Vi App and log in with your number
Go to "Help & Support" section from the menu
Type keyword
"VAS"
in search box
